## The question this task had to settle

Whether the paper's central mechanism claim holds: that sinks form at predictable positions (an "address"), that the address is a property of training rather than of one model, and that SAGA's learned gate relates to it. The five sub-questions below are the task's own.

## How to read these numbers (read this before quoting any)

An adversarial review of the first Phase-C pass returned 11 confirmed findings; two of them had inverted a reported result. Both are now modelled explicitly, and neither reference is zero:

**1. Concentration has a finite-sample floor.** A *spatially uniform* ground truth does not produce Gini 0 when it is estimated from 10000 images. At ~20 sinks per image the null Gini is about 0.017; at ~0.02 sinks per image it is about 0.49. Sink mass spans three orders of magnitude across variants, so RAW Gini, entropy and top-k shares are not comparable between them. Every concentration statistic therefore carries its own null (`*_null`, a binomial null at that map's own mass) and an excess value (`*_excess`), and Q4 reports PAIRED differences of excess. Differencing raw values instead flips the sign of the registers result.

**2. The 196 positions are not 196 independent samples.** The maps are spatially smooth, so the iid Spearman reference (sd 0.0716) is far too generous. Every correlation carries an exact permutation p-value over the 8 dihedral x 196 torus-roll transforms of one map (1568 transforms, deterministic, no seed), which preserves both maps' autocorrelation. Under it the effective sample size is roughly 20, not 196 — so use the p-value, not the `n` column, which is the position count.

Significance below means p < 0.05 under that permutation null. Q5's per-run extremal layer is an argmax over 12 layers, so it also carries a Bonferroni-corrected p.

### 2. Is it seed-stable? Yes — this is the make-or-break number, and it holds.

Spearman between baseline maps of a cell's repeats, all pairs:

| cell | n pairs | mean rho | range | p range | pairs p<0.05 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ViT-S/mixup | 6 | 0.9121 | 0.8919 to 0.9365 | 0.0006 to 0.0019 | 6/6 |

Across all cells, 8 of 8 baseline pairs are significant. The ViT-S/mixup pairs span legacy runs and fresh seeded reruns months apart under different trainer code, so the address is a property of the training distribution, not of a particular model.

### 3. Is it recipe-stable? No.

| basis | n pairs | mean rho | range | pairs p<0.05 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| canon | 8 | 0.3051 | 0.1916 to 0.3989 | 2/8 |
| mad | 8 | 0.1342 | -0.0211 to 0.3118 | 0/8 |

Against a within-recipe mean of 0.9121, the cross-recipe value lies largely inside its own spatial null. This is not evidence that the address transfers between recipes.

### The two optional runs, and what they would buy

`e2r_vitb_mixup_baseline_s2` and `e2r_vitb_mixup_saga_s2` (ViT-B/16, mixup, seed 2, 300 epochs, ~29 h each on 4x A100) are prepared and unsubmitted. They address the ViT-B cell, which is the weakest part of this analysis, in four ways:

1. **Top-1 significance.** SAGA's ViT-B gain is +0.438 with SE 0.286 and `significant_2xSE = 0` — not significant, because its two paired deltas disagree (legacy-nomixdir +0.724, s1 +0.152). With four repeats the ViT-S/mixup equivalent is +0.456 with SE 0.212 and IS significant. A third ViT-B pair would settle it either way.
2. **Seed-stability is confounded, not just thin.** ViT-B's one pair compares a LEGACY run (old trainer, no seed control) with a seeded one, so it mixes seed variation with a code-version change. `baseline_s2` would give the first clean same-code, different-seed pair at ViT-B.
3. **The gate question at ViT-B is genuinely undecided** (Q5 above: nothing significant, and the two repeats differ in sign between bases). A third gate dump is the only way to break it.
4. **Q4 would gain a third pair** — least urgent, that result is already solid.

They do nothing for ViT-S, nothing for the true-nomix cell (also one pair), and nothing for registers, which needs its own prepared run `e2r_vitb_mixup_registers_s1`. Cost is real: ~58 h of 4x A100, competing with TASK-08 and TASK-09 for the queue. Nothing in TASK 07 is blocked either way — the limitations are recorded in the note's OPEN ITEMS.
