## 1. One paragraph

**SAGA (Spatially-Aware Gated Attention)** is a PhD project (Pattern Recognition Lab, FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg) targeting **ICLR 2027**. The method: a learnable, **input-independent, per-head, per-position** sigmoid gate applied to the attention (SDPA) output of a ViT, suppressing the high-norm "attention sink" artifacts supervised ViTs develop — no extra tokens (unlike register tokens), no content conditioning (unlike LLM-style gated attention). Gate size, verified from checkpoints (φ shape `[L,H,N]`): **14,112 params for ViT-S/16**, **28,224 for ViT-B/16**. (Older docs say "~2,352" — that predates the per-head gate and must never be reused.)

The project went through a full evidence rebuild: a code audit invalidated every originally recorded number, all salvageable results were re-derived from checkpoints with corrected metrics and provenance, and a seeded rerun matrix replaced the headline table. What follows is what survived.

## 2. The science

Supervised ViTs develop a few extreme patch tokens — huge L2 norms, attracting attention (Darcet et al., ICLR 2024, fixed them with *register tokens*). A separate pathology is **oversmoothing**: patch tokens collapsing toward mutual similarity. Different diseases; they can move in opposite directions.

SAGA multiplies `sigmoid(φ[layer, head, position])` onto the attention output of **patch tokens only** (CLS is never gated). φ is a plain learned table — no input dependence. Headline runs initialize φ = 0 (gate = 0.5); identity init (φ = +4) is untested and is the subject of the pending ablation.

**The central claim as it now stands:** sink formation in supervised ViTs is **positionally predictable** — sinks have a spatially concentrated, seed-stable *address* — and a tiny position-indexed gate learns to suppress precisely that address, improving accuracy and oversmoothing without relocating the sink function elsewhere.

**The claim that died:** the founding narrative ("+0.98 without MixUp, benefit vanishes with MixUp") does not exist. Two bugs produced it — validation reported one GPU's ~12.5k-image shard, and the config loader never applied the nomix augmentation block, so all legacy "nomix" runs trained *with* MixUp+CutMix and were merely extra repeats of the mixup cell. The corrected result is stronger and simpler: the gain is **recipe-robust**.

## 3. Established results

### 3.1 ImageNet-1k classification (`results/tables/e2_pooled.csv`)
300 epochs, full 50k val, `last` checkpoint; repeats listed before means; paired-by-repeat deltas.

| Cell | Repeats | SAGA Δ vs baseline | Significant (2×SE) | Registers Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ViT-S/16 mixup | 4 | **+0.46** (SE 0.21) | **yes** | −0.38 (n=2) |
| ViT-S/16 true-nomix | 2 | +0.33 / +0.32 | n=2, SE unreliable | — (none run) |
| ViT-B/16 mixup | 2 | +0.44 (SE 0.29) | no | −0.61 (n=1) |

True-nomix absolute accuracy sits ~5.5 pts below the mixup regime (baseline 73.0 vs 78.7) because the recipe is otherwise tuned for mixing — it is a **mechanism cell, not a headline cell**. Registers cost accuracy in every measured cell. (Two ViT-B/mixup seed-2 runs were submitted; if they completed, that cell becomes n=3 — check the log.)

### 3.2 Diagnostics (`e2_pooled.csv`, `sink_robustness*.csv`)
- **Oversmoothing: the most consistent result in the project.** SAGA improves it in every cell, every repeat, both metric variants, by ~25–40% (e.g. 0.312 vs 0.509 in ViT-S/mixup), with effective rank up. Registers worsen it in 3 of 4 cells (ViT-B: 0.817 vs 0.562).
- **Sink counts are metric-dependent, and that dependence is itself a finding.** Per-image median+5·MAD falls with the norm bulk (which SAGA compresses), so it can label SAGA "worse"; an absolute τ misreads globally hotter models (registers). Primary metric = **fixed τ calibrated per (arch, recipe) on that cell's baseline** (`fixed_thresholds_canon.json`), defended by the threshold sweep and norm histograms. Under it, SAGA < baseline in every valid cell.
- **Consolidation at ViT-B scale:** fewer corrupted positions but *larger* residual extremes (p99.9 +54%), reproducible across both repeats. Consistent with 2026 theory that sink mass is functionally necessary.
- **Relocation:** SAGA raises the CLS-norm ratio in 4/4 cells while *lowering* CLS attention share — norm relocation, not attention relocation.

### 3.3 The sink address (`results/tables/sink_address.csv`, `results/notes/sink_address.md`)
All correlations use an exact permutation null over 1568 dihedral/torus-roll transforms (the iid Spearman reference is far too generous); concentration statistics carry their own finite-sample nulls.
- **The address exists and is diffuse but real:** entropy near uniform, yet top-5 positions carry 2.66× (ViT-S/mixup) and 4.96× (ViT-B/mixup) their uniform share.
- **It sits on ring 1 — one patch inside the grid border** — in both mixup cells; the true-nomix cell is essentially flat.
- **It is seed-stable:** ViT-S/mixup baseline maps correlate ρ = 0.912 across 6 repeat pairs (8/8 pairs significant across cells), spanning legacy runs and fresh seeded reruns months apart under different trainer code. A property of the training distribution, not of one model.
- **It is not recipe-stable** (cross-recipe ρ ≈ 0.31, 2/8 significant): the border ring is created by the MixUp/CutMix recipe, not by ViT training as such.
- **SAGA preserves the address while emptying it** (ρ ≈ 0.87 ViT-S, 0.79 ViT-B, sinks removed, concentration tightened); **registers relocate and de-concentrate it** (ρ 0.477 ViT-S, 0.006 ViT-B).
- **The gate targets the address:** in ViT-S/mixup all four repeats pick layer 7 or 8, ρ = −0.52…−0.59, both threshold bases, all Bonferroni-significant. ViT-B and true-nomix are **undecided** (nothing survives correction; repeats disagree in sign).

### 3.4 Test-time registers baseline (`results/tables/T_ttr.csv`, `results/notes/ttr_baseline.md`)
Clean-room implementation (the authors' repo carries no license; method read at a pinned commit, no line copied — `third_party/ttr/PROVENANCE.md`). Validation gate frozen before use.

| Cell | Gate | Top-1 Δ vs baseline (95% CI) | **Δ vs SAGA** | Sinks removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ViT-S/mixup s1 | PASS | −0.212 [0.048, 0.376] | **−0.526** | 90.3% |
| ViT-S/mixup legacy | PASS | −0.192 [0.030, 0.352] | **−0.492** | 93.7% |
| ViT-B/mixup | FAIL | −0.976 [0.812, 1.140] | **−1.225** | 55.2% |
| ViT-S/nomix | FAIL | −0.058 | −0.224 | 41.5% (1.5 tokens) |

**TTR is below SAGA on top-1 in every cell.** Where it passes it *relocates* the address (ρ −0.09, −0.29, indistinguishable from zero); where it fails it *preserves* it (ρ +0.937) — an observed association across two architectures, not a mechanism. Disclosed limitation: the layer-3–12 restriction was chosen after a full-depth failure (the mid-layer range comes from the authors' paper; thresholds never moved).

### 3.6 Dense prediction — Gate 2: **PARTIAL** (`results/notes/gate2_report.md`)
One run per cell; no seed repeats, so no delta carries a standard error.
- **COCO:** AP **+0.427**, AP50 +0.334, AP75 +0.414, AP_M +0.703, AP_L **+1.164**, **AP_S −0.844**. Per category: SAGA above baseline in 57/80 on AP (median +0.408) but 43/80 on AP_S with **median +0.055** — the negative aggregate is carried by a few sparse categories (toaster −34.1, bed −20.0, bear −17.1). Within-run AP_S spread over the last three evals is 0.39–0.76, the same order as the delta.
- **ADE20K:** mIoU_ss −0.242, mIoU_ms +0.002 (a wash); background classes move for SAGA (wall +0.197, sky +0.114, floor +0.545); 69/150 classes above baseline.
- Registers rows are **not backbone-matched** (unseeded legacy backbone), and its segmentation run peaked at epoch 29 vs 79 for the others.

**Working hypothesis linking §3.5 and §3.6:** positional suppression helps where the suppressed positions are uninformative (planes against sky; large objects) and costs where class evidence lives there (birds in habitat; small objects, which disproportionately occupy border regions). **Untested** — the ring-ablation experiment (TASK 13) tests it directly and is the highest-value remaining analysis.

## 4. Why the evidence was rebuilt (the audit, in brief)
Original code had: shard-only validation (top-1 from one GPU's ~12.5k images), register tokens contaminating patch statistics, oversmoothing as consecutive-neighbour cosine on the last batch, a pooled outlier threshold, no seed control, repeats overwriting each other's checkpoints, an unmerged nomix augmentation block, and checkpoints saved only every 25 epochs under a 24 h walltime (which silently killed the ViT-B/mixup cell at epoch 74). Downstream fixes since: mIoU ignore-index, detection double-normalization, registers backbone path, fine-grained test-set selection. **No pre-audit number is citable.**

## 5. Goals and standards
1. **ICLR 2027.** Contributions: (i) the corrected two-axis diagnostic and threshold methodology; (ii) SAGA, a token-free positional intervention; (iii) the address mechanism — sinks are positionally predictable and the gate provably targets them; (iv) honest comparison against registers and test-time registers, both of which underperform it.
2. **Evidence standard:** every number traces to a file under `results/`; figures are drawn only from `results/figures_data/`; `REPRODUCE.md` maps every table and figure to run ids and commands.
3. **Honesty standard:** mixed and negative results (CUB, AP_S, the undecided ViT-B gate–address link) are reported with caveats, never hidden; single-repeat cells are labelled; void runs are never cited.
4. **Statistics:** paired-by-repeat deltas, mean ± std, "real" only if paired mean > 2×SE; n=1/n=2 cells flagged.
