## 4. What Track A does NOT settle

- **I1 is descriptive.** It measures where exceedances fall and how stable that arrangement is. It establishes no mechanism, and a map correlation between two checkpoints licenses a statement about two maps and nothing else.
- **The scale null tests ONE alternative explanation, not all of them.** It asks whether a single multiplicative rescaling of the norm field reproduces the variant's map. A rescaling that varied by position, or any other confound, is untouched by it.
- **I6 tests PRESENCE, not cause.** The nine public checkpoints differ from this cohort and from each other in training data, objective, augmentation, patch size, depth and input resolution simultaneously. A model that shows the pattern shows it; the recipe grouping is a pre-registered prediction about which models will, not an identified cause. Nothing is pooled across models or groups.
- **The permutation reference is approximate for a bordered map.** Every spatial p here assumes exchangeability under the 8 dihedral transforms and all torus rolls, and a roll wraps the border onto the interior. Every p is approximate in the same direction.
- **D5's masks are selected on discovery data.** That is what makes I4 non-circular, and it is also why the mask coordinates are an input to an experiment rather than a finding.
- **`docs/LOCKED_ANALYSIS.md` is still a DRAFT.** D5 is the last open decision; closing it and freezing the document are the human's acts.
