## The question this task had to settle

Figure 1 is the paper's first impression and nothing existed to build it from. TASK 07 had established the mechanism quantitatively — the sink address is a ring one patch inside the border, seed-stable at rho ~= 0.91, and in every ViT-S/mixup SAGA repeat the layer-7/8 gate is significantly *anti*-correlated with it. The teaser therefore had to show that mechanism **and carry a number**, so it could not be dismissed as three cherry-picked attention maps.

The number exists now. **It does not say what the task assumed it would.** See *Results*.

## How to read these numbers (read this before quoting any)

1. **The spread is over IMAGES, not over seeds.** Every cell is a single training run, so `2xSE` answers *"does this model attend differently on these images?"* — NOT *"would another training seed reproduce this?"* These are weaker than the TASK-06B/08 deltas, which do carry seed replication. Do not present them as equivalent.
2. **Every localization statistic needs its null.** `inbox_mass` is meaningless without `box_area_frac` (what uniform attention scores on the same boxes), and `ring1_mass` without the ring's area fraction. TASK 07 established that these statistics can invert sign once the null is accounted for. Both nulls are in the table as their own rows.
3. **Architectures are never pooled.** A ViT-S model is paired only against the ViT-S baseline. There is no ViT-B registers run.
4. **The three displayed images are illustrations, not evidence.** Every number is a population mean; no number in the figure or the note is computed from the shown images.
5. **Attention is not attribution.** CLS->patch attention in the last block is one readout of where a model looks. It is not a saliency method and carries none of their guarantees.

## What the evidence base actually is

### The frozen probe set (`results/probe/probe_set.json`)

| group | n | status | sha256 | what it is for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `curated` | 12 | frozen | `460598123a23` | the 12 Figure-1 candidates; NEVER the basis of a statistic |
| `random200` | 200 | frozen | `5f15c8457b26` | the population for every ImageNet statistic |
| `boxes20` | 20 | frozen | `9097dd577866` | the population for the in-box metric |

Frozen forever and write-once **per group**: each carries a sha256 over its own canonical serialization, and building a later group re-verifies the earlier ones rather than trusting them. Paths are relative, never dataset indices.

Curated criteria (4 images each, class-level priors):

- **`single_dominant_centered`** — ILSVRC classes whose val images are typically ONE large, roughly centred object against a plain or shallow field.
- **`small_or_offcentre`** — Small-animal/insect classes: the labelled object usually occupies a small, often off-centre part of the frame.
- **`textured_cluttered_background`** — Scene/landscape classes: no single figure-ground object, high-frequency texture over most of the frame.
