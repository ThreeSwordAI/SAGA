## OPEN ITEMS

- Seed-stability rests on ONE pair in ViT-S/nomix (n=1) and ONE in ViT-B/mixup (n=1); only ViT-S/mixup has four repeats (6 pairs). The TASK-07 A2 optional runs (e2r_vitb_mixup_baseline_s2 / _saga_s2) would raise ViT-B to 3 pairs.
- Registers is represented by LEGACY repeats only in every cell and is absent from ViT-S/nomix; no registers run has yet gone through the seeded trainer (the 2-epoch smoke passed, the chains are unsubmitted).
- The 196 positions are NOT 196 independent samples: the spatial-permutation null sd is 0.1804 to 0.3417 against an iid reference of 0.0716, i.e. an effective sample size around 10 to 32. The `n` column on a correlation row is the POSITION count, not that effective size; use the p-value.
- MONTE-CARLO NOISE, not ties, is what makes a sparse map's ranking meaningless: these maps have so few sinks that one position's frequency carries a relative standard error above 0.5, so their concentration, ring profile and correlations are not comparable with the other cells — ViT-B/mixup registers legacy-nomixdir [canon] rel SE 0.92.
- Q5 is reported at each run's extremal layer. The full per-layer profile is in the CSV for baseline-matched, own as spearman_layer00..spearman_layer10 (the spatially constant final layer has no row), and for every comparator in Faddr.npz (`profile__*`).
- Concentration nulls are Monte-Carlo (see the `*_null` rows for sims and seed); correlation p-values are exact permutation tests and need no seed.
- Both threshold bases are reported for every question; no basis has been designated primary for the address question.
