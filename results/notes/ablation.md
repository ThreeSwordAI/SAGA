## 3. Q2 — does the init matter (E vs F)?

F − E = **+0.240** top-1.

But the arms' own gate dumps say the question is not clean. Arm F starts at
sigmoid(+4) = 0.982 and ends at a mean gate of **0.6163**, against arm E's **0.5441**.
phi = 0 is a fixed point of decoupled weight decay while phi = +4 is not, so arm F is
pulled toward arm E's regime throughout training by the weight decay both arms share
(`wd_phi_zero: false`, the shipped e2r treatment of phi). The arms may differ only in
`gate_mode` and `gate_init_logit`, so this was left matched deliberately — but it means
**F is not a clean test of initialisation**; it is a test of initialisation under a decay
that erases it. Reading F as an init preference would overstate what was measured.

## 5. Diagnostics

### The primary sink metric SATURATES in this cell and orders nothing

Under the committed ViT-S/mixup canon tau (20.8516) every arm counts essentially all 196
patch tokens as sinks: A 196.0000, B 195.9180, C 195.9790, D 195.9999, E 195.9632, F 195.9988. That is above TASK-02B's own saturation flag (>= 95% of
the patch tokens), and a threshold that every token clears cannot rank anything. The
differences between those numbers are the last decimal of a saturated count and are not
evidence about any arm.

**The cause is a schedule mismatch, not a fault in the runs.** The canon tau was
calibrated on the 300-epoch `e2r_vits_mixup_baseline_s1`, and these models are 100-epoch
models with a different norm scale: arm A's own last-block patch norms have a median of
37.28 against that tau of 20.85, and its own per-image MAD threshold (the quantity the
canon definition is built from) is 52.95 — read from `diag_final_last_normstats.json`,
fields `p50` and `mean_threshold_mad_k5`. A tau calibrated on this cell would sit near
the latter, i.e. roughly 2.5x the one applied here.

**Nothing was recalibrated.** TASK-12's acceptance list requires the canon tau be taken
from the ViT-S/mixup cell and never recalibrated, so it was not, and the saturation is
reported instead of worked around. The consequence has to be stated plainly: **this
ablation cannot answer the sink question on its primary metric.** Answering it would need
a threshold calibrated on THIS cell's own arm-A baseline, declared under its own key
with its own provenance — a decision for the human, not a substitution to be made here.

What remains is the secondary MAD metric, and it is confounded against precisely the
comparison at issue: PROJECT.md §3.2 records that per-image median+5·MAD falls with the
norm bulk, and a uniform 0.5 gate compresses exactly that bulk. So arm B scoring well on
MAD is the predicted behaviour of the metric, not evidence about the mechanism. Both
numbers are below; neither settles anything.

On MAD, the frozen arm B (4.4334) and the learned spatial arm E (4.6851) both sit far
below the baseline (11.7130). As above, that is what this metric does when the norm
bulk is compressed, so it is not evidence that the two interventions are equivalent —
and it is not evidence that they differ either. The question is open and this cell,
as calibrated, cannot close it.

Context for reading these: the 300-epoch baseline of this cell sits at oversmoothing
0.4314 and effective rank 89.75 (same file, n=4). A 100-epoch model of the
same architecture is a less pathological object than a 300-epoch one, so there is less
for any gate to recover here. That is a property of the cell, not a defence of an arm.

## 6. Does the learned gate track this cell's sink address?

Correlations use TASK-07's EXACT permutation null (8 dihedral × 196 torus rolls),
imported from `analysis/address_analysis.py`, never the iid Spearman reference — for maps
this smooth the iid null is far too generous. The extremal layer is an argmax over 12,
so its p is Bonferroni-corrected.

**The `canon` basis carries no address map in this cell.** The same
saturation as §5: every position is a sink in every image, so freq is exactly 1.0 at all
196 positions and the map has no spatial structure to correlate against. Those rows
carry no rho, deliberately. Only the MAD basis remains, so unlike TASK-07 — which
required a result to hold in BOTH bases — nothing here can be cross-checked between them.

| arm | basis | extremal layer | rho | p (exact) | p (Bonferroni) | null sd |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E | mad | layer_absmax=7 | -0.7461 | 0.00128 | 0.01403 | 0.2582 |
| F | mad | layer_absmax=8 | -0.5901 | 0.00702 | 0.07717 | 0.2798 |

TASK-07 found this gate-vs-address relationship on the 300-epoch ViT-S/mixup runs at
**layers 7-8, negative, rho −0.518…−0.594, Bonferroni-significant in 4/4 repeats**
(`results/notes/sink_address.md`). Here:

- arm E reaches rho **-0.7461** at layer 7, Bonferroni p 0.0140 — same sign, same depth.

That is an independent replication of the mechanism finding on a different schedule,
and it is the strongest result in this ablation. Its limits are equally concrete: one
seed, one basis only (the canon map is degenerate here, so the two-basis agreement
TASK-07 required cannot be checked), and a correlation is an alignment — not a
demonstration that the alignment is what produces the accuracy.

Arms B, C appear in the table with **no correlation value, deliberately**: their
gate is constant across positions by construction, so a rank correlation against any
map is *undefined*, not zero. Printing 0.0 there would read as "the non-spatial gate
ignores the address", which would be a statement about arithmetic rather than about the
model.

Individual layers of the SPATIAL arms are blank for the same reason and no other: arm E at layer(s) 11; arm F at layer(s) 11. A layer that never moved from its init is spatially constant, so its rho is undefined too — that is one layer, not the arm.

## 7. Caveats — all of them apply to every number above

1. **One seed per arm.** No delta here carries a standard error, and none of the project's
   significance machinery applies. The headline cell's own per-repeat deltas at 300 epochs
   span -0.126 to +0.890 — wider than every arm-to-arm difference measured here.
2. **100 epochs, not the headline's 300.** `warmup_epochs` stays at 20, i.e. 20% of this
   schedule against 6.7% of the headline's; it was not retuned, because the arms had to
   stay identical to each other and to `e2r_vits_mixup_*` in everything but the gate.
   Cross-schedule comparisons of absolute top-1 are not meaningful.
3. **One cell.** ViT-S/16, mixup recipe. Nothing here speaks to ViT-B or to true-nomix.
4. **Arm D is standard LayerScale, which scales every token including CLS**, while the gate
   never touches CLS; and its gamma carries the same weight decay phi does. Both are
   deliberate (it is the control a reviewer means), both are deviations from the gate's
   treatment, and both are recorded in `configs/abl_matrix.yaml`.
5. **Arm F's initialisation does not survive its own schedule** (§3), so it under-tests the
   init question rather than answering it.
6. **The primary sink metric saturates here** (§5), so the mechanism half of this
   ablation is unanswered rather than answered either way. The accuracy half (§2-4) and
   the gate-vs-address correlation (§6) are unaffected — they do not depend on that
   threshold.
