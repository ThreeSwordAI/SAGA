## 0. The legacy e6 numbers are superseded and VOID

The legacy fine-grained headline deltas (**+2.19 Aircraft, +1.29 CUB**) are **VOID and are never cited**.
`evaluation/e6_finegrained/tools/train.py` used the official TEST
split as its validation set, selected `best.pth` on it, and
evaluated every 5 epochs, so the reported peak was tuned on test
(bug B7); it also loaded backbones with `strict=False` without
checking unexpected keys. Those numbers are superseded by the table
below and must not be compared against it: they measure a different
(invalid) quantity, not a weaker version of the same one.

## 1. Protocol actually used

- Val split: a frozen, committed, seeded stratified 10% carve of the
  OFFICIAL TRAIN split (`results/ftsplit/<dataset>_val_split.json`);
  `best.pth` selected on it, checked EVERY epoch.
- The official TEST split is touched EXACTLY ONCE per run, after
  training, with the val-selected `best.pth` loaded ->
  `eval/test_final.json`. Second evaluations are refused by the
  trainer.
- Backbones: the seeded e2r mixup s1 checkpoints, strict-loaded
  (zero missing/unexpected keys) with the sha256 asserted against
  `configs/ft_matrix.yaml`.
- Hyperparameters mirror the legacy e6 trainer (AdamW backbone
  1e-5 / head 1e-3, wd 0.05, cosine to 1e-7, 100 epochs, batch 64,
  label smoothing 0.1, grad clip 1.0, 224px).

## 3. Sanity checks

**Val curve peaked before the final epoch?** 16 of 16 runs peaked strictly before their final epoch; 0 peaked at (or one before) the last epoch.

Best epochs range 11..86 out of 100 trained. Selecting on the held-out val split therefore changed which weights were evaluated for these runs — the final-epoch weights were not the val-best weights.

**Val vs test disagreement > 2 pts:** 6 of 16 runs flagged.

| run | val top-1 @ best | test top-1 | val − test |
|---|---|---|---|
| cub ViT-B baseline f0 | 84.333 | 80.549 | +3.784 |
| cub ViT-S saga f1 | 83.500 | 80.532 | +2.968 |
| cub ViT-S saga f2 | 82.833 | 80.152 | +2.681 |
| cub ViT-S baseline f1 | 83.167 | 80.739 | +2.428 |
| aircraft ViT-S saga f0 | 76.000 | 73.687 | +2.313 |
| cub ViT-S saga f0 | 83.167 | 80.860 | +2.307 |

All 16 gaps have the same sign convention (val − test); range -0.407 .. +3.784, mean +1.553. 13 of 16 are positive (val optimistic relative to test).

Scale for reading those gaps: the val splits are small, so their
own sampling noise is of the same order as the flag threshold.
- CUB-200-2011: val n = 600 ⇒ binomial SE ≈ 1.53 pts at the cell's mean val accuracy 83.2%
- FGVC-Aircraft: val n = 700 ⇒ binomial SE ≈ 1.65 pts at the cell's mean val accuracy 74.2%

A val-selected maximum over many epochs is additionally biased
upward relative to an independent test estimate, so a positive
val − test gap of this size is expected and is not evidence of a
protocol fault. It is reported here, not corrected for.
