## 3. Read this before signing

Two things about branch 3 that the sentence in §1 does not say.

**(a) The branch turned on a small margin.** Branch 1 requires every primary
diagnostic to retain at least 0.70 of its native gap under the
terminal-gate bypass. 2 of 5 did not:

- `cos_all`: survival 0.680, short of 0.70 by **0.020**
- `cos_nosink_mad`: survival 0.694, short of 0.70 by **0.006**

The other 3 are `eff_rank` 0.877, `count_fixed_cal` 0.914, `count_mad` 0.909. Across all five, survival runs 0.680–0.914. Any cutoff at or below 0.67 would have returned branch 1 instead. The 0.70 was fixed before these numbers existed and is not being moved now; it is recorded here so the reader can see how much of the verdict rests on it.

**(b) The branch-3 boilerplate overstates what these numbers show.** The
sentence in §1 ends "the historical final-block diagnostic distinction was
substantially a terminal-gate effect", which is the wording task file §7
attaches to branch 3. On this evidence the terminal gate accounts for the
SMALLER part of the gap: bypassing it retains 0.68–0.91 of every native gap, with every sign preserved.
Moving the readout back one block is what actually removes the difference, and
only for two of the five: `cos_all` keeps 0.09, `cos_nosink_mad` keeps 0.20 at `s11_out`, while `eff_rank` keeps 0.73, `count_fixed_cal` keeps 0.98, `count_mad` keeps 0.98.
So on this evidence the STAGE, not the gate, carries most of the cosine
difference, and the other three diagnostics are largely unchanged by either.
Both facts are in the table above. **The verdict `D1 = s11_out` is unaffected**;
what is in question is only the clause explaining WHY — and that clause is the
one that would be quoted in the paper.

A wording that the same table supports without the overstatement:

> *D1 = `s11_out`: on the calibration split, in the `vit_small|mixup` cell (n = 4 pairs), the
> SAGA-vs-baseline gap at the historical stage survives a terminal-gate bypass at
> 0.68–0.91 of its native size with every sign preserved, so the untrained terminal
> constant is a contributor but not the main one; the cosine diagnostics, however, retain
> only 0.09–0.20 of that gap one block earlier. The final-block patch diagnostics are
> therefore a mixture of what training did and what a readout-invisible constant does,
> and they are reported with the `term_1.00` and `s11_out` controls beside them, never alone.*

Signing either one is the human's call. What must not happen is the §1
